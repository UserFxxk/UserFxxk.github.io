RFC 9116 SECURITY POLICY

Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure & Ethical Testing Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Maintainer: Fata Fatikhul Karim (fatafatikhul@gmail.com)

1. Outbound Research Ethics (How I Conduct Security Assessments)

All security research, reconnaissance scripts, and vulnerability root-cause analyses published on this domain strictly adhere to the following rules of engagement:

  • Written Authorization & Scope Compliance: Testing is performed exclusively on systems with explicit owner permission or within official Bug Bounty program scopes (e.g., HackerOne).
  • Non-Destructive Verification: Zero production denial-of-service (DoS), zero automated brute-forcing, and zero unauthorized alteration of other users' data. If a verification probe creates test records, all record UUIDs are documented and reported immediately to the maintainer for cleanup.
  • Redaction & Confidentiality: Public case studies redact proprietary client names, domains, and internal identifiers while preserving the technical Root-Cause Analysis (RCA) and remediation patterns.

3. How to Report & Response SLA Targets

To report a vulnerability or security configuration issue in any in-scope asset, email fatafatikhul@gmail.com with the subject line [SECURITY DISCLOSURE] <Repository/Asset> and include reproduction steps or a Proof-of-Concept.

  • Initial Acknowledgment: Within 48 hours.
  • Triage & Severity Assessment: Within 5 business days.
  • Patch & Public Credit: Coordinated fix release within 14 days, with credit in the repository release notes.
